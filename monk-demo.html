01 / Measure

An electrical fingerprint

The proposed AD5933 instrument measures tissue response at several frequencies. Magnitude describes the size of the impedance; phase describes the timing difference between voltage and current.

02 / Learn

Compare three approaches

Monk-LR uses Ridge regression. Monk-RF averages decision trees. Monk-GB builds trees that correct earlier errors. The simplest model that performs well on unseen specimens is a useful starting point.

03 / Validate

Keep specimens separate

Leave-one-sample-out evaluation trains on other specimens and tests on a specimen excluded from training. Real experiments and an independent final batch are needed to establish performance.

04 / Interpret

Make the score transparent

The current concept maps age to an index: 100 × (1 − age / 48), clipped to 0–100. Fresh, Moderate, and Spoiled are illustrative age bins; they are not experimentally validated freshness or safety labels.
